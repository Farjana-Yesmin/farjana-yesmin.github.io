Projects

MedHE: Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning for Healthcare

A novel framework combining adaptive gradient sparsification with CKKS homomorphic encryption to enable secure, communication-efficient collaborative learning on sensitive medical data with HIPAA compliance.

Federated Learning Homomorphic Encryption Healthcare Privacy CKKS Differential Privacy Gradient Sparsification

Key Results:
• 97.5% communication reduction (1277 MB → 32 MB per round)
• 89.5% accuracy (comparable to standard FL, p=0.32)
• Differential privacy with ε ≤ 1.0
• Scalable to 100+ medical institutions

AI Chatbots for Healthcare in Bangladesh
