Paper Accepted at MobiHealth 2026 – Heraklion, Greece

My paper "Fairness‑Aware Representation Learning for ECG‑Based Disease Prediction in Wearable Systems" (co-authored with Nusrat Shirmin) has been accepted as a full paper at the 13th EAI International Conference on Wireless Mobile Communication and Healthcare (MobiHealth 2026). Paper Accepted at ICAIHE 2026 – Waseda University, Tokyo

My paper "Explainable AI for Maternal Health Risk Prediction in Bangladesh: A Hybrid Fuzzy-XGBoost Framework with Clinician Validation" (co-authored with Nusrat Shirmin and Suraiya Shabnam Bristy) has been accepted as a full paper for publication and presentation at the 2026 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence for Health and Education (ICAIHE 2026). The conference will be held at Waseda University, Tokyo, Japan on July 8-10, 2026. The paper presents a novel hybrid framework combining fuzzy logic interpretability with XGBoost, validated through clinician feedback (N=14), and includes fairness analysis across Bangladesh's eight divisions.

Projects

MedHE: Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning for Healthcare

A novel framework combining adaptive gradient sparsification with CKKS homomorphic encryption to enable secure, communication-efficient collaborative learning on sensitive medical data with HIPAA compliance.

Federated Learning Homomorphic Encryption Healthcare Privacy CKKS Differential Privacy Gradient Sparsification

Key Results:
• 97.5% communication reduction (1277 MB → 32 MB per round)
• 89.5% accuracy (comparable to standard FL, p=0.32)
• Differential privacy with ε ≤ 1.0
• Scalable to 100+ medical institutions

AI Chatbots for Healthcare in Bangladesh
