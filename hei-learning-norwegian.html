Learning from Mistakes – Reviewing & Reinforcing

I have been diligently working through my "30 recent mistakes" reviews. Some of the key sentences and translations I have recently practiced include:

  • "He doesn't like his house." – Han liker ikke huset sitt.
  • "We're eating pasta and soup." – Vi spiser pasta og suppe.
  • "A girl is eating rice." – Ei jente spiser ris.
  • "Gutten og jenta spiser pastaen." – The boy and the girl are eating the pasta.
  • "My boss speaks Norwegian at home." – Sjefen min snakker norsk hjemme.
  • "Ta en dag om gangen." – Take one day at a time.
  • "His brother's birthday is on the seventh of May." – Broren hans har bursdag den syvende mai.
  • "The festival is tomorrow." – Festivalen er i morgen.
  • "We're at a music festival." – Vi er på en musikkfestival.
  • "My father doesn't like jogging." – Faren min liker ikke å jogge.
  • "Du er en god venn." – You are a good friend.
  • "His uncle's birthday is on the ninth of September." – Onkelen hans har bursdag den niende september.
  • "Kommer han fra Sverige?" – Does he come from Sweden?
  • "Do you understand English?" – Forstår du engelsk?
  • "Hvordan smaker pommes fritesen?" – How does the fries taste?
  • "We still need people." – Vi trenger fortsatt folk.
  • "Det starter åttende juni i år." – It starts on the eighth of June this year.
  • "My coffee is already cold." – Kaffen min er allerede kald.
  • "Lager hun frokost?" – Is she making breakfast?
  • "Svømmer du alltid i helgen?" – Do you always swim on the weekend?
  • "Toget kommer om omtrent fem minutter." – The train arrives in about five minutes.
  • "I unfortunately have to work." – Jeg må dessverre jobbe.
  • "Jeg drar ofte på telttur i skogen." – I often go camping in the forest.
  • "Elsa og Mari, spiller dere tennis?" – Elsa and Mari, do you play tennis?
  • "Han har allerede en hund." – He already has a dog.
  • "Jeg har ikke lyst, og jeg må jobbe." – I don't want to, and I have to work.
  • "Drar du til matbutikken i dag?" – Are you going to the grocery store today?
  • "Hvem er guttene?" – Who are the boys?

Reflections on One Year of Norwegian

Surpassing 371 days feels surreal. When I started, I couldn't have imagined this level of consistency. The language has started to feel like mine. I am no longer just memorizing flashcards; I am expressing my thoughts, reading menus, and understanding conversations.

The shift toward speaking practice has been a game-changer. Shadowing and recording myself continue to expose my weaknesses, but they also highlight how far I've come. I am actively looking for opportunities to speak Norwegian outside of the app, and the vocabulary I am learning is directly applicable to real life.
